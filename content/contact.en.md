## Describe your need in writing

The **contact form** is the most direct route if your request is already clear in your mind. A few lines will do: the context, the question you want answered, and the kind of data you have.

Do not worry about getting the statistical part wrong — rephrasing it with you is precisely my job.

## Or talk it through

If your situation is harder to write than to explain, book a **30-minute introductory call**. It is free, commits you to nothing, and is usually enough to work out whether I can help and how.

## What happens next

1. **I reply** within a few working days.
2. **We scope the need**, in writing or on the call. This step is free.
3. **I write you a proposal**: what I propose to do, how, and by when.
4. **You decide.** Nothing starts before you agree.

If your request falls outside what I do, I will say so at step 2 rather than waste your time.

## Good to know

- I work **in French or English**, anywhere in **Belgium**.
- Your data stays confidential; a non-disclosure agreement can be signed before anything is sent.
- For questions about rates, location or organising a training session, use the form: it depends too much on context to answer in advance.
