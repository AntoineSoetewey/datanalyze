## Cookies and trackers

**This site uses no cookies.** It stores nothing on your device: no cookie, no local storage, no session identifier, no tracking pixel.

Nor does it call external resources that could track you without your knowledge: there is no Google Fonts stylesheet loaded from a CDN, no social media button, no embedded map and no embedded video. The contact form and the booking link are **plain outbound links** — precisely so that no third-party cookie is set during your visit.

There is therefore no separate cookie page on this site: there would be nothing to put on it.

## Analytics

**No analytics tool is installed.** No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Matomo, none at all. I therefore do not know how many people visit this site, nor which pages they read.

The only information available to me is:

- the **aggregated statistics from Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**, which show which queries the site appears for in search results — these tools store nothing on your device and give me no individual data;
- the **technical logs of the host** (Netlify), kept briefly for security and operational reasons, which I can only see in aggregate.

## Data you send me

### Contact form

If you use the [contact form](/en/contact/), you are taken to a form hosted by **Airtable**. The data you enter there (name, email address, description of your need, and anything else you choose to add) is passed to me so that I can answer your request.

- **Purpose**: answering your request and, where relevant, preparing a proposal.
- **Legal basis**: your request itself — pre-contractual steps taken at your initiative (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Recipient**: me alone. Your data is never sold, shared or used for marketing.
- **Retention**: for as long as needed to handle your request, then for the duration of the working relationship if one begins, and at most **three years** after our last contact.

### Booking a call

If you book a call, you are taken to **Calendly**, where you provide your name, email address and preferred slot.

- **Purpose**: arranging the call.
- **Legal basis**: pre-contractual steps taken at your initiative.
- **Retention**: same as for the contact form.

### Data entrusted during an assignment

If we work together, you will send me data to analyse. It falls under a separate regime:

- I act as a **processor** under the GDPR, with you remaining the controller;
- the data is **used only for the agreed assignment**, and for nothing else;
- it never leaves my workstation and is passed to no third party;
- it is **deleted at your request** at the end of the assignment;
- a **non-disclosure agreement** can be signed before anything is sent;
- for health data or other sensitive data, a **data processing agreement** under Article 28 GDPR is put in place, and sending pseudonymised data is preferred.
