faq:

    a: |
      No, that is the normal situation. Reshaping, merging sources and handling
      inconsistencies are an integral part of the assignment. It is better to give me
      access to the data as it is than to a "cleaned" version whose cleaning
      decisions I cannot see.
  - q: "Does this kind of project need artificial intelligence?"
    a: |
      Rarely, and that is good news. On business data of typical size, a classical
      statistical model performs just as well, explains itself in one sentence and
      needs no special infrastructure to maintain. I propose a complex method only
      when it delivers something simple methods do not.
  - q: "What happens after the assignment?"

      ongoing support, that is agreed at scoping — but it should be a choice, not a
      dependency you are stuck with.
  - q: "Do you work on site?"

  - q: "Can we start small?"

      shows you what your data genuinely supports, and saves you committing to a
