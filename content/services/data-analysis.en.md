faq:
  - q: "What format should I send my data in?"
    a: |
      Excel, CSV, SPSS, Stata, SAS, Access or a database: all of these work. Do not
      spend time reorganising a file before sending it — cleaning and reshaping are
      part of the job, and an "already tidied" file sometimes hides useful
      information from me.
  - q: "My data is confidential. How do you protect it?"
    a: |
      I work with health data routinely. Data never leaves my workstation, is used
      for no other purpose, and is deleted at your request when the assignment ends.
      A non-disclosure agreement can be signed before anything is sent. For patient
      data, send it already pseudonymised where possible: I almost never need
      identities.
  - q: "How long does an analysis take?"
    a: |
      It depends mostly on the state of the data and the number of questions. A
      focused analysis on a clean dataset is a matter of days; a full study with
      cleaning, modelling and writing is a matter of weeks. I give you a firm
      estimate after scoping, before you commit.
  - q: "Can I list you as a co-author on a publication?"
    a: |
      That is possible and it happens regularly when the methodological contribution
      is substantial. It is discussed at scoping: some prefer a straightforward
      service, others a genuine scientific collaboration. Both work for me.
  - q: "What if my data cannot answer my question?"
    a: |
      I will tell you, and I will tell you early. It is a common situation — too few
      subjects, a missing variable, an unsuitable collection design. You would rather
      hear it at scoping than after several weeks of work, and I would rather say it
      than produce a result I do not believe in.
