faq:

      This service covers the phase where you have your data and you are analysing

      [study design](/en/services/study-design/): that is the only moment when the
      design, the variables and the sample size are still fixable. Most requests do
      arrive mid-analysis or after a review, though, and there is almost always
      something to be done.
  - q: "Do you run the analysis for me?"
    a: |
      Not under this service: here you keep control and I secure the method. If you
      would rather delegate entirely, the [data analysis](/en/services/data-analysis/)
      service is the one you want.
  - q: "Can I consult you about a single question?"
    a: |
      Yes. Many requests are settled in one session: a choice of test, an
      interpretation of output, a review of a methods section. There is no minimum
      duration.
  - q: "Do you work with software other than R?"
    a: |
      Methodological advice does not depend on the software: I can support you
      whether you work in SPSS, Stata, SAS, Python, Jamovi, JASP or Excel. When it comes to writing
      or reviewing code, though, I do that in R.
  - q: "My field is not medical. Is that a problem?"
    a: |
      No. My own research was mostly in biostatistics and actuarial science, but the
      methods carry across: social sciences, economics, biology, engineering,

      the label on the discipline.
